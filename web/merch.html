ShopSmart merchandising console
Smart merchandising · rules engine on Couchbase

Prices that follow stock, the clock and the market. On every phone.

The engine rebuilds one small rules document per fulfillment center whenever an input changes: inventory, the hour, a competitor price. Couchbase stores it, Sync Gateway pushes it to every phone served by that center, and the app prices the cart on the device with the same arithmetic the backend uses. Change an input below and watch a signed-in phone re-price within seconds, signal or not.

The pricing clock

Promotions tied to quiet and peak hours. Click an hour to move the demo clock; phones use the same hour, so the breakfast promotion appears and expires on the device.

Shelf prices at

Base member price before tier discounts and personal offers. Overstock (4x the normal level or more) is marked down; nearly sold-out and high-demand items leave every promotion; a competitor price within % of list is matched automatically.
